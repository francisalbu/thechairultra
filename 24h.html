.solo.duos.crews

is a 24-hour last-person-standing race, in the heart of the city. You run as many loops as you can, .solo, in .duos or in .crews of 4. It's competition, it's community, and it's your chance to go further than you ever have.

Every hour on the hour.

All athletes leave the start box together on the hour, every hour.

6.7 km per loop.

Athletes have one hour to run the loop at their own pace. They must be back in the start box before the next hour starts.

Late means disqualification.

Athletes who aren't in the start box when the next hour begins are disqualified.

Two ways to win.

Last Person Standing wins. The race continues hour after hour until only one runner remains.

.25th lap - Winner takes it all. If more than one runner makes it through 24 hours, there's one final loop. First to finish it wins.

Your race.
Your strategy.

.solo

It's just you against your personal best.

  • Last person standing wins. If it goes past 24 hours, all remaining .solo runners dispute a 25th loop to decide the .solo winner.
  • Runners can bring their supporting crew.
.duos

Pick your teammate and go beyond together.

  • Define a strategy and take turns between loops.
  • Last duo standing wins. If it goes past 24 hours, pick who runs the 25th loop to decide the .duos winner.
  • Runners can bring their supporting crew.
.crews

Your team is your crew, no external assistance.

  • Define a strategy and take turns between loops.
  • Every runner must run a minimum number of loops.
  • Last crew standing wins. If it goes past 24 hours, pick who runs the 25th loop to decide the .crews winner.
