The concept

Redefine your distance.

The Chair is a project designed to redefine endurance racing by challenging athletes to push beyond what they believe they are capable of.

We believe distance is more than the kilometres you can run. It is the gap between what you think you can achieve and what you can actually do when you refuse to quit.

That is the distance you will redefine.

What sets us apart

Up until now, last person standing races only had 2 possible conclusions. Last Person Standing and DNF (did not finish).

The Chair Ultra races provide a mental stimulus for runners to keep on going.

Introducing the DNL(Did not leave)

If:

  • You stayed in the comfort of the chair and didn't go to the corral.
  • You walked up to the corral, crossed the start line, did some meters and turned around.

You are a .DNL

Runners who .DNL ring the quitting bell, seated in the chair at the start of the corral.

Questions.

What is a backyard ultra?

A race format where every athlete runs the same 6.7 km loop, starting together every hour, on the hour. Miss the start and you're out. The last athlete standing wins.

Do I need to be an ultra runner?

No. You only need to be able to run 6.7 km in under an hour. How far you go after that depends on how long you refuse to quit.

What is a .DNL?

Did Not Leave. When the bell rings, you either don't start the next loop, or you start it and turn back after the first 20 metres. The chair took you out.

What is a DNF?

Did Not Finish. You didn't complete the loop before the hour was up, so the clock took you out.

Can I have a support crew?

Solo runners can have a support crew. Duos can have one person. Crews of four run with no outside support.
